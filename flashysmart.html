What makes SACard different

Everything you need to actually retain a language long-term

SM-2 Spaced Repetition

Cards are scheduled using the proven SM-2 algorithm so you review words exactly when you're about to forget them — maximising retention with minimal study time.

AI-Generated Content

Add a single word and instantly receive 7 example sentences, synonyms, antonyms, translations, and native-speaker audio — all generated by AI.

Deck Organisation

Group cards into topic decks — travel, food, business, and more. Manage multiple learning goals without mixing them up.

Progress Statistics

Visual stats show your daily streak, cards learned, accuracy rate, and upcoming reviews so you always know where you stand.

10+ Languages

Supports over 10 languages including Arabic, French, Spanish, Japanese, German and more, all with matching audio pronunciations.

Secure Auth & Sync

JWT authentication backed by Supabase (PostgreSQL). Your decks and progress sync across devices and are safely stored in the cloud.

Push Notifications

Daily reminders nudge you to keep your streak going. Notifications are smart — they only fire when you actually have cards due for review.

Dark Mode

Full dark mode support for comfortable late-night study sessions, following your system preference automatically.
